Move Court

An AI agent panel decides where you should move after school

Jake Bozlee · MGT 409, Fall 2026

The problem

Every graduating student faces one of the highest-stakes decisions of their life -- where to live -- with almost no practice, and pressure from parents, partners, and friends who want different things for them.

Who has it: every graduating student weighing multiple cities, job offers, or "stay near family vs. chase career" tradeoffs.

What's new

  • Niche / Livability: generic, unweighted category scores
  • Teleport / Numbeo: raw data, no reasoning
  • Generic chatbot: no structure, no dataset, no visible reasoning

Move Court interviews you, shares context between agents, shows you each agent's live reasoning, surfaces conflicts with people in your life, and makes the outcome concrete with a Future You narrative.
